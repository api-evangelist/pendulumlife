Written by: Taylor Berggren, MS, RDN In nature, a keystone species is one that has an outsized impact on its environment relative to its abundance. Think of wolves in Yellowstone or bees in an ecosystem. Remove them, and the entire system shifts—often in ways that are hard to reverse.

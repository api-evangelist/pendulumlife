Written by: Jennifer Agha-Khan, MS, RDN Reviewed by: Christina O'Connor, RD There’s no denying that stress can wreak havoc on the gut. We’ve all felt it—whether it’s a high-stakes week at work, back-to-back travel, recovering from illness, or just too many meals eaten on the go. The bottom line: when we’re stressed, our gut feels it too.

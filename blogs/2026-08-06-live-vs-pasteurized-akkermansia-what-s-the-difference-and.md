Written by: Christina O'Connor, RD Senior Healthcare Account Manager, Pendulum Therapeutics Not all Akkermansia supplements are created equal. Some contain live bacteria, capable of colonizing your gut and getting to work. Others contain bacteria that have been heat-treated until they're no longer alive — considered a postbiotic, not a probiotic.

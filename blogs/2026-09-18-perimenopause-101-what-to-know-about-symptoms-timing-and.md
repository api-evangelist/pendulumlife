Reviewed by: Adam Perlman, MD, MPH Perimenopause doesn't get a lot of airtime compared to menopause itself, but it's often the harder stretch. It's the hormonal transition where estrogen and progesterone start to fluctuate — sometimes wildly — before your period stops for good. For a lot of women, it's also the most confusing stretch, because the symptoms don't always look like what you'd expect from "the change." Here's what perimenopause actually is, what's normal, and how to know what stage you're in.
